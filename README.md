## Who's steering this

I run **[NovaSoft RW](https://novasoftrw.vercel.app)** out of Kigali, turning ideas into shipped products — web platforms, APIs, and ML-driven features that hold up outside a demo. My working style is simple: prototype fast, cut what doesn't earn its place, ship what does. Right now I split my time between client builds, my own product line, and teaching myself the deep-learning stack one architecture at a time.

Everything I make and everything I'm learning lives at **[hamedprodev.onrender.com](https://hamedprodev.onrender.com)** — think of it as my workshop floor, not a polished brochure.
